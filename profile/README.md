<h1 align="center">Verify Everything</h1>

<p align="center"><b>∀x. verified(x).</b><br>Trust, but verify. Actually, skip the trust.</p>

---

We collect libraries that have been **formally verified**: machine-checked against a stated specification. Each one ships with its proof, a pipeline that re-checks it, and a claim card that says exactly what was proven and what wasn't.

### Get involved

- **Verified something?** Open a *Submit a library* issue on [verified-libs](https://github.com/Verify-Everything/verified-libs/issues/new/choose).
- **Want to verify something?** Browse the open [entry issues](https://github.com/Verify-Everything/verified-libs/issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3Aentry) that are still *Not started*.
- **Opinions on what "verified" should mean?** The [standards](https://github.com/Verify-Everything/verified-libs/blob/main/STANDARDS.md) are a draft, and good arguments change them.

<sub>Found an unsound or overstated claim? Please report it privately. See our <a href="https://github.com/Verify-Everything/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md">security policy</a>.</sub>
